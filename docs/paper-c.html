Let f(1)=1,\quad f(n)=\prod_p f(p)^{v_p(n)}, where the prime values are independent fair signs: this is the completely multiplicative Rademacher model.

Deterministic copies such as f(2x)=f(2)f(x) persist at every scale, so classical mixing heuristics cannot justify independence of distant windows.

Dyadic and macroscopic localization, linear algebra over \mathbb F_2 and small-prime conditioning control the weighted relations. Exact one-word forcing at private largest-odd-prime pivots supplies the growing signed field.

01Theorem 3.1 ↗Proposition 3.25 ↗

A weighted relation profile at exponent 5/3

Fix 0<\delta<1, 0<\beta_-<\beta_+, \varepsilon>0 and \beta_-\log M\le B=L+1\le\beta_+\log M. For ordered separated starts in U=[\lceil M^\delta\rceil,M)\cap\mathbb Z, let \rho_L(x,y) be the dimension of the row-relation space of the stacked run matrices over \mathbb F_2. Then

\begin{aligned}R_{2,\delta}(M,L)&:=\sum_{\substack{x,y\in U\\|x-y|>L}}(2^{\rho_L(x,y)}-1)\\&\ll M^\varepsilon\left(M^{3/2}2^{B/6}+M2^{2B/3}+M^{2/3}2^B\right).\end{aligned}

In particular, R_2(N,L)\ll_{C,\varepsilon}N^{5/3+\varepsilon} on a dyadic block when |L-\log_2N|\le C. The same profile holds for prescribed signs. Capping each relation weight by T\ge1 gives, uniformly in T,

R_{2,\delta}^{[T]}(M,L)\ll M^\varepsilon\left(M^{3/2}2^{B/6}+M2^{2B/3}+M^{2/3}\min\{T,2^B\}\right).

The cap acts on the dense terminal contribution. It preserves the raw 5/3 exponent; the remaining sufficient dictionary exponent 1/2 comes from the nonterminal term.

Why the marginal ceiling changes the dictionary range

At a good site, distinct dictionary words are mutually exclusive. With a=m2^{-B},\ \Lambda=Na, their union indicator satisfies

\mathbb P(I_x^{\mathcal W}I_y^{\mathcal W}=1)\le\min\{a,a^2 2^{\rho_B^{\rm val}(x,y)}\}.

Choose T=a^{-1}=N/\Lambda. The normalized arithmetic contribution becomes

N^{-1/3+\varepsilon}\left(\Lambda^{11/6}m^{1/6}+\Lambda^{4/3}m^{2/3}+\Lambda\right).

The terminal term is independent of the dictionary size at fixed intensity. Neither exponent is asserted to be optimal. For prescribed signs, the full valuation kernel omits the two block-parity constraints and obeys the same capped estimate.

02Theorem 1.3 ↗Theorem 5.1 ↗

Poisson fields for growing deterministic dictionaries

Let \mathcal W_N\subset\{-1,+1\}^{B_N} contain m_N distinct prescribed words, and set I_{x,w}=\mathbf1\{f(x-1+j)=w_j\ (0\le j<B_N)\}. For fixed C>0 and 0<\eta<1/2, assume

m_N\le N^{1/2-\eta},\qquad |B_N-\log_2(Nm_N)|\le C,\qquad \Omega(\mathcal W_N)\longrightarrow0.

Then the complete site–word field admits the lattice comparison

d_{\rm TV}\!\left(\mathcal L((I_{x,w})_{x\in I_N,w\in\mathcal W_N}),\ \bigotimes_{x\in I_N,w\in\mathcal W_N}\operatorname{Pois}(2^{-B_N})\right)\longrightarrow0.

The total intensity is \Lambda_N=Nm_N2^{-B_N}. All word identities and locations are retained; deterministic restrictions are allowed. The field is also asymptotically equivalent in total variation to the corresponding field of independent fair signs.

The overlap hypothesis
\Omega(\mathcal W)=\frac1m\sum_{w,v\in\mathcal W}\sum_{d=1}^{B-1}2^{-d}\mathbf1\{(w_d,\ldots,w_{B-1})=(v_0,\ldots,v_{B-d-1})\}.

Both self-overlaps and directed cross-overlaps contribute. The matrix tester computes this exact finite quantity. A small value for one dictionary does not establish an asymptotic hypothesis for a sequence of dictionaries.

Typical dictionaries: averaging the distance after the dictionary is fixed

Independently of f, sample a uniform m-element dictionary in the binary cube. In a fixed positive logarithmic band for B, with intensity Λ=Nm2−B bounded above and below, let ΔN(W) be the mean conditional total-variation distance for the full site–word field, with W held fixed inside that law. The mean averages over the conditioning field \mathcal F_{Y_N^\sharp}. For every fixed 0<ε<1/3,

\mathbb E_{\mathcal W}\Delta_N(\mathcal W)\le r_N,\quad r_N\ll e^{-V_N+o(\nu_N)}+N^{-1/3+\varepsilon},\quad \mathbb P_{\mathcal W}\{\Delta_N(\mathcal W)>t\}\le\min\{1,r_N/t\}.

This typical theorem has no uniform m≤N1/2−η ceiling; it includes m of order N. The same conclusion holds for uniformly sampled full-row-rank affine dictionaries. It does not certify every chosen dictionary or permit adaptation to f.

03Theorem 1.2 ↗Theorem 7.10 ↗

An unconditional boundary–bulk crossover

Let R_M be the longest constant run in the prefix, and X^*_{M,L} the earliest contained length-L window. Write \alpha_L=2^{-\pi(L)},\ \lambda_M=M2^{-L}. Under L\to\infty,\ L=O(\log M),\ \lambda_M\to0,

\mathbb P(R_M\ge L)=\alpha_L+\lambda_M+o(\alpha_L+\lambda_M).

If s_{M,L}=L-\log_2M-\pi(L)\to s\in\mathbb R, then

\mathcal L\!\left(\frac{X^*_{M,L}}M\;\middle|\;R_M\ge L\right)\Longrightarrow \frac1{1+2^{-s}}\delta_0+\frac{2^{-s}}{1+2^{-s}}\operatorname{Leb}_{[0,1]}.

The boundary branch localizes at start 1 and has positive sign. Its exact cylinder mass is \alpha_L; its asymptotic conditional weight is the logistic function w_\partial(s)=(1+2^{-s})^{-1}. The proof needs relative bulk comparison and microscopic localization, beyond normalizing these two reference masses.

04Theorem 7.11 ↗Equation (7.28) ↗

Geometric overshoots in two different clocks

On the initial-run event C_L=\{f(1)=\cdots=f(L)=+1\}, let J_\partial=\min\{j:f(p_j)=-1\}, \ell_\partial=p_{J_\partial}-1 and G_L=J_\partial-\pi(L)-1. Exactly, for k,h\in\mathbb N_0,

\mathbb P(G_L=k\mid C_L)=2^{-k-1},\qquad\mathbb P(\ell_\partial-L\ge h\mid C_L)=2^{-[\pi(L+h)-\pi(L)]}.

In the bulk, the limiting mark counts additional integer positions: \nu_{\rm geo}\{e\}=2^{-e-1}. Under the rare-intensity hypotheses above, the joint law of source, lattice start, sign and source-specific overshoot satisfies

d_{\rm TV}\!\left(\mathcal L(\Gamma_{M,L}\mid R_M\ge L),\ Q_{M,L}\right)\longrightarrow0.
The full moving-weight comparison law

Fix 0<\delta<1, set I^{\rm bulk}=[\lceil M^\delta\rceil,M-L+1]\cap\mathbb Z, \lambda^{\rm bulk}=|I^{\rm bulk}|2^{-L} and \nu_\pm=(\delta_{-1}+\delta_{+1})/2. Then

\begin{aligned}Q_{M,L}={}&\frac{\alpha_L}{\alpha_L+\lambda^{\rm bulk}}\,\delta_{(\partial,1,+1)}\otimes\nu_{\rm geo}\\&+\frac{\lambda^{\rm bulk}}{\alpha_L+\lambda^{\rm bulk}}\,\delta_{\rm bulk}\otimes\operatorname{Unif}(I^{\rm bulk})\otimes\nu_\pm\otimes\nu_{\rm geo}.\end{aligned}

The state \Gamma_{M,L} includes an exceptional symbol for starts outside the initial and bulk branches. No convergence of the source-weight ratio is required. This is total variation on a common discrete space; the diffuse spatial limit in Result 03 is weak convergence. The bulk geometric law is asymptotic, whereas the displayed boundary-clock identities are exact.

05Theorem 7.7 ↗

A complete signed field at growing intensity

The separate critical sign-resolved field theorem

Fix C>0. Uniformly for |L-\log_2N|\le C, let Kx,eσ record a run starting at x∈[N,2N), with full length L+e and sign σ. On the common countable lattice,

d_{\rm TV}\!\left(\mathcal L((K_{x,e}^{\sigma})_{x,e,\sigma}),\bigotimes_{x,e,\sigma}\operatorname{Pois}(2^{-L-e-2})\right)\le\exp\!\left[-\left(\frac1{\sqrt2}+o_C(1)\right)\sqrt{\log N\log\log N}\right].

The moving count mean is N2−L. A diffuse spatial Poisson limit is weak, along converging intensities; the displayed total-variation comparison keeps the exact grid.

Theorem 1.1 ↗

Set L=\lfloor\log_2 M\rfloor-a_M,\quad a_M\in\mathbb N_0,\quad a_M=o(\log M),\quad n=M-L,\quad\Lambda=n2^{-L}\to\infty. For 1≤j≤n, let Ij,e,σ indicate a maximal run starting at j+1, with full length L+e and actual sign σ. Its base is contained in the observed prefix; the excess may continue beyond M.

For A_M\in\mathcal F_{Y_M^\sharp} of positive probability, write \mathcal I_M=-\log\mathbb P(A_M). Assume fixed c>0 and the one-factor budget

\mathcal I_M+\log\Lambda\le V_M-c\nu_M.

Then, for every 0<c′<c and 0<ε<1/3,

d_{\rm TV}\!\left(\mathcal L((I_{j,e,\sigma})\mid A_M),\bigotimes_{j,e,\sigma}\operatorname{Pois}(2^{-L-e-2})\right)\ll e^{-c'\nu_M}+M^{-1/3+\varepsilon}.

Every site, full excess and actual sign are retained on the same grid, without a spatial partition penalty. The run starting at 1 is excluded. Every common measurable readout inherits this error bound.

Exact hard scales and proof mechanism
\frac{e^{u(\nu)}}{u(\nu)}=\nu,\quad u(\nu)\ge1,\quad D(\nu)=\nu u(\nu)-\operatorname{Ei}(u(\nu)),
V_M=D(\log M/V_M),\quad\nu_M=\log M/V_M,\quad Y_M^\sharp=\lfloor e^{V_M}\rfloor.

These are the positive large-M solutions of (4.4). The coupling forces one exact word by changing private largest primes with odd valuation, then charges the sites actually changed through reciprocal-pivot estimates and Palm–Stein comparison.

06Corollary 7.8a ↗

An empirical count law after fixing the random function

Fix 0<α<1 and τ>0. At the prescribed dyadic scales, put

M_k=2^k,\quad L_k=k-\left\lfloor\frac{\alpha V_{M_k}}{\log2}\right\rfloor,\quad n_k=M_k-L_k,\quad h_k=\lfloor\tau2^{L_k}\rfloor.

Hold f fixed and vary only the origin u of an hk-site window of potential left-maximal run starts. Let μkf be the empirical law of those counts over all origins 0≤u≤nk−hk. For almost every f,

d_{\rm TV}(\mu_k^f,\operatorname{Pois}(\tau))\to0,\qquad h_k=M_k\exp\{-\alpha V_{M_k}+O(1)\}=M_k^{1-o(1)}.

The pair (α,τ) is fixed, the scales are dyadic, and both signs are aggregated. The statement concerns counts: two windows with different configurations can contribute to the same count bin. A fixed strip offers only finitely many local configurations, so its complete location vector obeys a different comparison:

\liminf_k d_{\rm TV}\!\left(\widehat\mu_k^f,\bigotimes_{i=1}^{h_k}\operatorname{Pois}(2^{-L_k})\right)\ge1-e^{-\tau}(1+\tau)>0.

How the arithmetic reaches the probability law

Prime exponents modulo two encode the constraints. The critical and dictionary comparisons use a conditional dependency graph. The growing signed field uses an exact one-word coupling and the sites it actually changes.

Encode words over F₂. Remove the rational-relation subspace before counting residual dependencies.

02Weighted arithmetic

Private prime coordinates, bounded-height Runge–Pell arguments and shifted kernel energy control all nonzero relations.

03Remaining prime signs

Condition on small primes. Private coordinates give exact marginals. Use a dependency graph or the selected-pivot coupling, according to the field being compared.

04Poisson transfer

Check local overlaps separately. Cap dictionary union probabilities; for the growing run field, charge the coupling’s directed change sets.

Why does the cap allow a larger dictionary?

Write the dictionary size as m=N^\alpha, with fixed positive intensity \Lambda=Nm2^{-B}. A contribution with a negative power of N decays. The cap removes the dictionary-size factor from the terminal contribution; the nonterminal term then limits the sufficient range.

At leading order, the uncapped calculation requires α < 1/3; the capped calculation allows α < 1/2. The ε = 0 view omits the arbitrarily small exponent loss: it is not an ε = 0 theorem, and neither equality endpoint is included.

These are sufficient upper-bound ranges, not optimal thresholds. A nonnegative exponent means this bound does not establish decay; it does not mean the Poisson approximation is false. The complete theorem still requires the length band, the overlap condition \Omega(\mathcal W_N)\to0 and control of the other error terms. The cap does not improve the raw relation exponent 5/3.

Notation at a glance

Long runs unless indicated otherwise. Logarithms are natural when no base is written.

f(n)
Completely multiplicative random sign. Prime signs are independent and fair; every square has sign +1.
I_N
The integer block [N, 2N). N is the number of candidate sites.
B=L+1
A run-start word contains one preceding opposite sign and L constant signs. General dictionary words have length B.
R_2
Weighted sum of nonzero square relations for ordered separated pairs of windows.
\rho_L(x,y)
Dimension of the row-relation space over F₂ for the stacked run matrices.
\mathcal F_Y
Information supplied by the signs at primes p ≤ Y.
d_{\rm TV}
Total variation distance: the supremum of probability differences over measurable events.
\Omega(\mathcal W)
Weighted directed suffix–prefix overlap, including self-overlaps.
\pi(L)
Exact number of primes ≤ L.
\alpha_L=2^{ -\pi(L)}
Exact probability of the positive initial run of length at least L.
\lambda_M=M2^{-L}
Bulk reference intensity in a prefix of size M.
s=L-\log_2M-\pi(L)
Crossover coordinate comparing the exact boundary cost to the bulk intensity.
G_L
Number of further positive primes after L and before the first negative prime, conditional on the initial run.
