The published archive freezes commit b9fa9ba490f201b408a302a86f1594ee620b228b, with Lean 4.34.0 and Mathlib v4.34.0. It contains the source-to-manuscript correspondence, source files and verification records.

This record concerns Paper C and its companion; it does not assert a Lean formalization of LPF, Arithmetic Clusters or Moving Prime Environments.

Formal scope and retained literature inputs

The registered families cover critical Poisson fields and transfers; word dictionaries and marked clusters; threshold staircases and Poisson–Gaussian limits; boundary and longest-run laws; microscopic–bulk crossover; microscopic and empirical fields; and Palm comparisons with cumulant obstructions.

  1. Scalar Poisson Stein factors.
  2. Finite Arratia–Goldstein–Gordon process comparison.
  3. Prime number theorem remainder.
  4. Multivariate Poisson Stein solution bounds.
  5. Laishram–Shorey prime-divisor estimate.
  6. Shorey’s square-product exclusion.
  7. Nicolas–Robin divisor bound.

The analytic Stein input for companion F.2 is assumed in the formal theorem; the printed semigroup construction is outside that proof. Verification establishes selected formal statements under their hypotheses; it does not discharge those literature inputs or constitute independent human peer review.
